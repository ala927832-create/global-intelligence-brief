### Three sequential corrective rounds, maximum three owner-facing review milestones

1. **Round 1 — Source discovery + G0 hard preflight.** Before writing any readable editions, identify at least **20 distinct, current, date-provenanced global events**, from at least **4 original publisher domains** and **4 editorial topic groups**. Deduplicate original canonical URLs and essentially identical event titles (agency reprints are not new events). Each countable event requires a real title, original HTTPS URL, valid UTC-aware publication time, and substantive source-attributed summary (at least 50 characters) or matching source-id evidence points. A headline alone is never a substantive story. Review source availability, feed failures, region coverage, ranking biases and data cutoff drift. If any criterion fails: `HOLD_SOURCE_INPUT_INCOMPLETE`; emit **diagnostic metrics only** and **do not invoke Gemini, render HTML/EPUB or hand off samples**. No fabricated filler, extra unapproved source provider or unauthorized paid API.
2. **Round 2 — Editorial depth and source-linked article production.** Morning Essential must have **10–12 substantial primary news articles + 10–12 different short news briefs (20–24 distinct events)** plus **4 distinct substantive analyses**. Morning Deep adds **1–2 current-headline-linked, primary-evidence-rich studies** with dated research, mechanism, history, conflicting interpretations and falsifiers; old standalone history is not counted as a current deep study. Identify every substantial claim's source. Gemini must materially revise in its two-pass flow; reject unsupported expansion and repeated wording. Evening remains tied to verified incremental news and its previously approved reading targets.
3. **Round 3 — One integrated P2 acceptance and handoff.** The **same canonical, fact-reviewed edition** must satisfy source provenance, headline cross-reference, numerical/date/quote review, distinct story/section checks, meaningful independent evidence, plagiarism/duplication QA, internal HTML navigation, EPUBCheck/offline resources, and **human-measured** Essential **30–40 min** and Deep **40–50 min**. Then submit one consolidated acceptance report and both readable editions. If failing after three internal rounds, report unresolved specific causes honestly; never claim completion or relax standards to make a green checkmark.

**No quantity-by-padding:** Source indexes, URL counts, snippets, duplicated story syndication, AI-generated paragraphs without attributable evidence, historical essays mislabeled as today's deep research, and boilerplate disclaimers **do not count** toward news count or research depth. Under-source-content cases must appear as internal diagnostics; a valid but incomplete snippet is not an independently checked full article.

**Release control:** Fixture-based automated unit tests may intentionally render internal samples but must be labeled `FIXTURE_ONLY_NOT_FOR_OWNER` and never escape into owner-review or publication artifacts. A green technical CI run is not editorial acceptance. P2, P3, P4 and public publication require their distinct signoffs; **publication remains OFF**.
