## Mandatory 90/100 critical-quality threshold
Six dimensions scored 0–100 by documented evidence: news selection 20%; provenance 20%; research 25%; numerical/T03 15%; editorial readability 10%; technical and reliability 10%.
Stage PASS requires weighted total >=90, each dimension >=85, each of four focal essays and each deep study >=90, all existing V3 hard gates passed, zero P0/P1 defects, independently verifiable T03, six genuine test-time source snapshots for R1 content, independently tracked deadline reliability for P3, and required human signoffs. No weighted score may override a missing hard gate or fabricated data. 85–89 is FAIL. Every rating includes evidence refs, reviewer role, deduction rationale and after-fix results.
