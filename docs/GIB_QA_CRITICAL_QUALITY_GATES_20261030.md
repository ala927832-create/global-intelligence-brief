## Six mandatory critical reviews
1. R1 major-news omissions: compare selected 20–24 deduplicated actual events against dated major international headlines, record selections and evidence-grounded exclusions, source diversity, topic/geographic coverage. No filler or industrial bias.
2. R2 substantive research: audit four distinct focal essays and 1–2 contemporaneous headline-linked deep studies for independent documents, mechanisms, historical comparison, counterevidence, falsification and measurable watch items. No padded prose.
3. R2 T03 calculation: trace numerator, denominator, unit, base date, observation period, source version, equation, assumptions, uncertainty and independent recalculation. Unresolved scope blocks approval.
4. R2 Gemini revision: inspect first draft, fact-check packet and second draft; require two material evidenced improvements, not superficial wording edits. Failed calls or no edits are HOLD.
5. R2/R3 independence: separate drafting, mechanical fact checking, adversarial editorial review and actual human signoff. Model scoring alone is not a human signature.
6. R1/R3/P3 deadline and delivery: validate each approved capture slot using actual capture job, start/end timestamps, qualifying snapshot artifact and its contents. ## Mandatory 90/100 critical-quality threshold
Six dimensions scored 0–100 by documented evidence: news selection 20%; provenance 20%; research 25%; numerical/T03 15%; editorial readability 10%; technical and reliability 10%.
Stage PASS requires weighted total >=90, each dimension >=85, each of four focal essays and each deep study >=90, all existing V3 hard gates passed, zero P0/P1 defects, independently verifiable T03, real deadline evidence and required human signoffs. No weighted score may override a missing hard gate or fabricated data. 85–89 is FAIL. Every rating includes evidence refs, reviewer role, deduction rationale and after-fix results.
